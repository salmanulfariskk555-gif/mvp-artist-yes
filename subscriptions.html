NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about creator memberships.

A membership is a way to support a creator and access exclusive content or experiences offered through their Kaalaverse membership.
Membership billing options will depend on the creator's available plans. Monthly and yearly options can be offered once subscription billing is enabled.
Depending on the creator's plan, members may receive exclusive photos, videos, tutorials, updates and other creative content.
The platform is designed to support creator-defined membership tiers, benefits and pricing. Creator dashboard and backend integration are required to manage live plans.
Membership cancellation will be managed through the customer account once subscription management is connected to the backend and payment provider.
