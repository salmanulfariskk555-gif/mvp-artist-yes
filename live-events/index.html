Some useful information about events and ticket enquiries.

Select the Enquire button on an event card and submit your contact details. The page prepares an enquiry message for you to send.
Online ticket booking and payment will be available after the ticketing backend and payment gateway are integrated.
The platform is designed to support event organisers. Event submission, verification and publishing require the organiser dashboard and backend.
Digital tickets and QR-based event entry validation require backend ticket generation and scanning functionality.
