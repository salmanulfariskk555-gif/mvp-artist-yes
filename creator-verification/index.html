Before Applying

Prepare Your Information

The following are examples of information that may be requested depending on your creator category.

Possible Verification Requirements

  • Full legal name and contact information.
  • Appropriate identity verification documents.
  • Creator profile and professional biography.
  • Portfolio, website or social media profile.
  • Artwork samples and ownership information.
  • Relevant performance or professional credentials.
  • Event organiser or business authorisation details, where applicable.
Privacy and Security Notice: Do not email sensitive identity documents or financial information through an unverified channel. The final platform must provide a secure document submission process and explain how verification information is stored, accessed and retained.
