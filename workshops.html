Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information about workshops and registration.

Browse the workshop categories and use the filters or search box to explore available learning categories.
Use the enquiry button on a workshop card to prepare a registration enquiry. Final registration will depend on the organiser's confirmed process.
The format will depend on each instructor. Online or in-person details should be confirmed in the individual workshop listing.
Artists and instructors can join Kaalaverse and express interest in listing their workshops. Publishing and management require the organiser tools and backend.
Online registration payments will require backend integration and a supported payment gateway.
